## 2. Where TradeWave Sits + the Positioning Wedge

TradeWave is **mid-market inside the real seasonality category**, not below it. Against the two verified comps it is priced at-or-below the comp on every rung while delivering an AI score neither competitor has.

- **Navigator $19 (no AI)** undercuts Seasonax Basic ($49.95, also no AI) by ~60% - the right relationship, since Navigator is the no-AI breadth tier and TradeWave *also* has a true $0 tier Seasonax lacks.
- **Analyst $47** lands almost exactly **on Seasonax Basic ($49.95)** - same money, but Analyst adds the AI-calibrated probability score, the per-year shows-its-work ledger, Sharpe/sample-size, the 100-Year/PE-cycle lens, Seasonal Market News + weekly Q&A.
- **Strategist $129** sits just above Seasonax Pro ($100) and just below Financhill's ~$132/mo quarterly-equivalent, undercutting Financhill's $199/mo monthly and its $199/mo seasonality-standalone - while adding all 15 markets + AI + live Zoom.

**The wedge: "The only seasonality engine that shows its work AND scores the odds."** Three pillars no single verified competitor combines:

1. **AI-calibrated probability score** - Seasonax has none at any tier; Financhill's "historical accuracy %" is raw frequency its own FAQ disclaims as not predictive. TradeWave calibrates raw frequency *down* with ML. Starts at $47, at/under Seasonax Basic.
2. **Shows-its-work per-year ledger** - every historical year green-up/red-down, losing years left in. Seasonax exposes win-rate + outlier filtering but not a transparent per-year record as the core artifact; Financhill collapses history into one projected number.
3. **Election (PE) cycle + 100-Year Pattern** as a first-class lens - Seasonax offers only a generic year-filter; Financhill has no regime overlay at all.

Customer-facing line (provider-neutral per brand rule - **never name competitors in copy**): *"AI-scored seasonality that shows every year - and a real free tier."* Competitor names stay internal only.
