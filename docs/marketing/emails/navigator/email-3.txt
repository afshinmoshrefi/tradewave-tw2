THE FOUR THINGS TO CHECK ON EVERY PATTERN

The year grid. Green years won, red years lost, and the losers are left in on purpose. A clean run of green with one or two reds is a different story than green-red-green-red. Look at the shape, not just the count.

% Profitable. How often the pattern finished up. Won 19 of the last 20 years is a fact about history, not a promise about next time.

Sharpe ratio. Return earned per unit of volatility along the way. Higher is steadier - a pattern that ground out its gains beats one that whipsawed to the same finish - so it is worth knowing before you size anything.

Sample size. Five years and twenty years are not the same evidence. More years behind a pattern means more to lean on.
