How to Read the Evidence Without Fooling Yourself

A strong-looking pattern is an invitation to investigate, not an instruction to act.

TradeWave’s research loop is SCAN, VALIDATE, ORGANIZE, then ACT. The decision always remains yours.

The opportunity table helps you scan. Before trusting any row, validate these four things:

1. THE YEAR GRID

Green years finished profitably. Red years did not. The losing years remain visible on purpose.

A mostly consistent record with one or two failures tells a different story from an alternating green-red record. Read the shape, not only the total.

2. PERCENT PROFITABLE

This shows how often the pattern finished up historically. A pattern that finished profitably in 14 of 15 years is a historical fact, not a promise about year 16.

3. SHARPE RATIO

Sharpe compares return with the volatility experienced along the way. A higher value generally indicates a steadier historical path, but it should never be read alone.

4. SAMPLE SIZE

Five years and fifteen years are not equal evidence. A high percentage based on a small sample deserves more skepticism than the same percentage across a larger record.
