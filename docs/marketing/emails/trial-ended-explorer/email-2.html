TradeWave Explorer

Explorer works best as a focused research habit. Pick one Dow 30 ticker. For one seasonal window, check three things: how many years are in the sample, how often it was profitable, and whether the year-by-year results are steady or carried by a few outliers.

Save only a setup that meets your standards. Explorer can track five opportunities in one portfolio. You do not need AI scoring to decide whether a historical record deserves a closer look.

On the Wave Viewer, Remind Me above the bar chart is a shortcut: it saves the loaded pattern if needed and adds its start and end dates to Google Calendar. When the loaded pattern already has a reminder — including one created from a Portfolio Manager row — the control shows that the reminder is set.
