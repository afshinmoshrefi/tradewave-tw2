The Part of Analyst That Is Not Software

So far we have talked about the engine. But a real chunk of what you are paying for on Analyst is human, and it is the part most new subscribers forget to use. Here is what it is.

The Live Weekly Q&A
Each week we run a live Q&A session where you can ask about a specific pattern you loaded, why a high win rate sits next to a soft Sharpe ratio, how to read a window's losing years, or whether a setup is aligned or curve-fit. Bring a real pattern from your own screen. The fastest way people get good at reading the evidence is asking about their own.

Want in? Reply to this email and we will send you the invite, with the join link and the time, before the next session. The fastest way to be sure you do not miss one is to watch your inbox for the weekly invite.

Seasonal Market News
Analyst unlocks our Seasonal Market News articles - the running written record of what the data is showing across markets and cycles. It is context for the numbers you are already pulling, not noise on top of them.

Email Support
When something does not make sense, you can email a human who knows the methodology. Not a bot, not a forum thread - a reply.

The Honest Catch
This human layer is a clear reason to be on Analyst rather than Navigator - and the easiest thing to pay for and never touch. So we will say it straight: if you never attend the Q&A and never open the research, you are paying $47/mo mostly for a layer you are not using. If the AI score and total-market breadth are not pulling their weight for you either, Navigator at $19/mo covers the three big indices and custom start dates for less, and there is no judgment in choosing it. (Email support is a safety net; you do not need to use it for it to be worth having.)

Our ask is simple: use the human layer once this week before you decide. Join one Q&A, read one article. If it earns its place, keep it. If it does not, downgrade - we will help.
