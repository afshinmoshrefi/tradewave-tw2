When You Should Stay Free, and When You Should Not

Plain talk: there are exactly three walls where the free seat stops being enough. If you have not hit one, do not pay us - a subscription you do not use is money we did not earn.

1. You want the odds scored for you. On free you read the record yourself (bars, % Profitable, Sharpe, sample size) plus the Trend Score (current direction). AI scoring adds a calibrated, model-read estimate of how reliable each setup has been - the line free does not cross. You can judge reliability by hand on free; the AI score just does that weighing for you. This is an Analyst feature.

2. You want to pick your own start day. Free shows today's windows; a custom start date is the cheapest unlock. 3. You want to hunt beyond the Dow 30. Free is Dow 30 only - if you keep wanting the same year-by-year hunt on a NASDAQ 100 or S&P 500 name, the cheapest plan covers exactly those big-index leaders; every US stock and ETF is a step further.

None of those true? Stay free - we would rather keep you for years than sell you a tier you will resent.
