How to read it

The Losing Years We Leave In on Purpose

A seasonal pattern is just three things: a symbol, a start day, and how long you hold (on free you are reading today's start-day patterns; picking your own start day is a later-plan move). The honest question is never "does it go up" - it is how often, how much, and how messy when it fails. TradeWave leaves the red bars on the chart, on purpose - the whole point of measuring instead of guessing.

Read three things together. The green/red bars (one per year, height = size of move) show whether the edge is broad or one giant year carries the average. % Profitable is a plain historical fact (19 of 20 years is a record, not a promise). Sample size and Sharpe tell you if the wins were smooth and repeatable or lumpy and lucky.

Compare two patterns side by side

Two names can both show a strong % Profitable and look identical - until you compare the bars and sample size and see one won steadily while the other rests on a single monster year.
