Breadth Is Safety, Not a Bigger Menu

Strategist's defining feature is breadth across all 15 markets: futures, forex, bonds, and crypto on top of every US stock and ETF, plus global equities and indices. It is easy to read that as a longer list. It is not. It is the only way TradeWave lets you diversify across asset class, the axis equities alone cannot give you.

Diversification has three axes

Timeframe - short tactical windows beside long compounding holds.

Sector - cyclical, defensive, commodity-sensitive names that do not all move together.

Asset class - energy futures, currencies, bonds, and crypto that answer to different drivers than equities. This third axis only exists for you on Strategist.

A portfolio that only works when one sector or one asset class leads is a bet, not a plan. When equities have no clean seasonal window, an energy future or a currency pair often does, driven by weather, harvest, or fiscal timing instead of earnings. Standing down on one market is easy when another is in season. That optionality across all 15 markets is the whole point of breadth.
