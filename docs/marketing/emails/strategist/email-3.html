A Pattern Is Not a Buy Signal. It Is Evidence to Read

The Opportunity Table ranks candidates. Ranked highly is not the same as approved. Every candidate earns its place only after you validate it in the Wave Viewer, in a fixed order. This is the discipline that turns the whole engine from a list into a decision.

The three views, in this order

1. Profit Bar Chart - one bar per year, green for up, red for down, losers left in. Is the edge broad-based, or carried by one hero year?

2. Trend Chart - the typical path. Where is the pivot zone, where is the recurring weak stretch? This is what keeps you from panic-exiting or overstaying.

3. Stats Table - the numbers confirm it: %Profitable, Sharpe, median versus average, cumulative return versus buy and hold. Act only when all three agree.

The One Judgment That Separates Edge From Noise

Read win rate with Sharpe. A high hit rate tells you direction. A low Sharpe means the path was lumpy, so stage in or hedge. A high Sharpe means clean, repeatable drift. And never trust a high percentage on a tiny sample - 91% across 11 occurrences is fragile, not exceptional.

Then check the path itself. MFE is how far it ran in your favor at the best point - your target. MAE is the worst drawdown you would have had to sit through - your stop and your invalidation line. TWR rewards windows that move strongly along the way, which Sharpe alone throws out. Seasonality is probability, not protection: a strong multi-year bullish window still posted a roughly -23% worst-point drawdown (MAE) before finishing near -5%. Define your exit before you enter, every time.
