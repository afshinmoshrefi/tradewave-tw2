02 / The Evidence

Read the Whole Track Record at a Glance

Open any pick and the same seasonal window is drawn across the lookback you set - ten years, twenty, or every year on record - one bar per year, counted the same way.

Each bar pairs the move in your favor that year with the heat it put you through first - the favorable run in green, the drawdown in pink, both shown for every year, none trimmed. A pattern that won 13 of 20 reads very differently from one that won 19 of 20. Underneath, the dashed gold line is the seasonal projection - where price has tended to travel across this window in past years, mapped onto the current one. A historical tendency, not a forecast.

Favorable run (MFE) - how far it ran in your favor    Drawdown (MAE) - how far against you first
