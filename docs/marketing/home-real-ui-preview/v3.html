02 / The Evidence

Open Any Pick and See Every Year - Wins and Losses Both

Same calendar window, drawn across every year of history, one bar per year, counted the same way.

The green band is how far price ran in your favor that year; the pink band is the drawdown it put you through first; a year that closed net negative shows a solid red bar - left in, never trimmed. Underneath, the price chart projects the seasonal path onto this year. A pattern that won 13 of 20 and one that won 19 of 20 are very different bets, and the Win% column counts the red years honestly so you see which you are looking at.

04 / The Model - It Ranks, It Never Predicts the Outcome

Machine Learning With One Honest Job: Ranking

The model orders the list. It never predicts the outcome.

One scan sweeps all 17 markets and surfaces the setups entering their seasonal window now. A separate gradient-boosted model reads dozens of features and does exactly one thing: orders the list. The seasonal statistics stay deterministic - the model only decides what you look at first. It calibrates the historical odds a pattern has repeated, validated on out-of-sample windows it never trained on, and never pretends to know how the year ends. A no-pattern result is a real finding, not weak support.
