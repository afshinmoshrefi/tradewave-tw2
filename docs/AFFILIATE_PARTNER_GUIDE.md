# TradeWave Partner Guide

This guide is for TradeWave affiliate partners and for any AI assistant a partner uses to plan,
write or check their promotion. It explains how the program works, what the partner dashboard
shows, how you get paid, and the rules every promotion must follow.

Your personal details (code, links, rates, payout method) are on your dashboard at
https://tradewave.ai/account/affiliate. The **Guide** tab there has a "Copy for my AI" button that
gives you this guide with your own details filled in at the top.

If this guide and your signed Affiliate Agreement ever disagree, **the Agreement wins**.

---

## 1. What TradeWave is (so you can describe it accurately)

- TradeWave is a **research tool** that finds seasonal patterns: price moves that have repeated
  around the same dates in past years, across 15 markets (US, Europe, Asia, futures, FX, crypto).
- Every pattern shows its evidence: year-by-year results, win rate, risk statistics and an
  ML win-probability score, so the reader can judge it for themselves.
- The daily AI pick has a public, forward-tested track record at https://tradewave.ai/scorecard.
- New accounts get a free 7-day trial of the full product. Current plans and prices are at
  https://tradewave.ai/pricing (prices change; always link there instead of quoting numbers).
- TradeWave is **educational and analytical information only**. It is not investment advice,
  not "signals", not recommendations, and it does not predict the market.

## 2. Your code, links and the audience discount

You have **one referral code** (for example `ANNE`). Your audience gets a discount and you get
credit when they use either of these:

| What you share | Example | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Your landing page | `https://tradewave.ai/join/ANNE` | A ready-made page with your name, photo and note, your discount, and a subscribe button. Best for most posts. |
| Your referral link | `https://tradewave.ai/?code=ANNE` | The TradeWave home page with your discount pre-applied at checkout. |
| Any TradeWave page | add `?code=ANNE` to the URL | Works the same way. |
| Your code itself | `ANNE` typed at checkout | Gives the discount and credits you. |

- **60-day window, first click wins.** The first time someone opens your link we store a cookie
  for 60 days. If they subscribe within 60 days, the sale is credited to you, even if they come
  back later through a plain link. If they had already clicked another partner's link first,
  that partner keeps the credit unless your code is used at checkout.
- **Discount size** depends on your terms (some partners have different discounts for monthly
  and annual plans). Your exact numbers are on your dashboard Overview.
- **No self-referrals.** Subscriptions bought with your own email or account never earn
  commission.

## 3. How commission works

- **Rate:** a percentage of what the customer actually pays (after their discount, before tax).
  Some partners have a different rate for monthly and annual plans. See your Overview tab.
- **Model:** one of three, shown on your Overview tab:
  - *Recurring (lifetime):* you earn on every payment for as long as the customer stays subscribed.
  - *First 12 months:* you earn on every payment in the customer's first year.
  - *First payment only:* you earn once, on the first payment.
- **Not commissionable:** taxes, free trials, 100%-off periods, and any payment that is refunded
  or disputed.
- **Refunds and disputes are taken back automatically.** If a customer is refunded, or a bank
  dispute takes the money back, the matching commission is subtracted in the month that happens,
  even if the original month was already paid. If a month ends negative, the difference is netted
  against your next payout (your Performance tab shows it as "carried forward").

## 4. Getting paid

1. Each month closes on the last day. On the **2nd** of the next month we calculate your
   commission and email you a statement (totals and a customer count, never customer identities).
2. You are paid **monthly by PayPal or Wise** to the account on file.
3. **A tax form is required before any payout:** IRS Form W-9 (U.S. persons), W-8BEN (non-U.S.
   individuals) or W-8BEN-E (non-U.S. entities). Email it to help@tradewave.ai. Your dashboard
   shows "Tax form needed" until we have it.
4. Small balances (under about $50 in a currency) may be held and added to a later payout.
5. You are paid in the currency your customers paid in. PayPal or Wise may charge their own fees.
6. To change payout details, email help@tradewave.ai. Changes are verified by hand because they
   control real money.
7. Questions about a statement must reach us within 30 days of it.

## 5. Your partner dashboard

Sign in at https://tradewave.ai/account/affiliate with the **same email address your agreement
was sent to**. (Signed up with a different email? Write to help@tradewave.ai and we will connect it.)

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Overview | Program status, your code and terms, your funnel (link visits, free signups, users in trial, paying subscriptions), this month's live estimate, lifetime paid and pending payout per currency. |
| Performance | The same funnel plus each settled month: net revenue, commission, distinct customers, status. |
| Payouts | Payout method, tax-form status, and every payout with its date and PayPal/Wise reference. |
| Links | Copy buttons for your landing page and referral link, plus links to any published Expert Desk takes. |
| Profile | Edit your landing-page name, note, sign-off and headshot (plain text only; we are notified of changes). |
| Guide | This guide, and the "Copy for my AI" button. |
| Expert Desk | Only if you were invited to write for Seasonal Market News (section 7). |

How to read the numbers:
- **Link visits** count each new browser that opens your link. Refreshes and repeat visits from
  the same browser are not counted again.
- **Free signups** are new TradeWave accounts created within 60 days of a visit to your link.
- **Paying subscriptions** are subscriptions credited to you at checkout.
- **This month (estimate)** refreshes about once an hour and can go down as well as up
  (refunds, disputes). The statement on the 2nd is the number of record.
- We never show who your customers are. Only counts and totals.

## 6. Promotion rules (summary of Agreement section 7)

Every promotion, including anything an AI writes for you, must follow these:

1. **Disclose the relationship every time**, clearly and near the link: for example
   "I earn a commission if you subscribe through my link" or "#ad / affiliate link".
2. **No performance or income claims.** Never say or imply that subscribers will make money,
   that TradeWave predicts the market, that a pattern "will" repeat, or that it removes risk.
3. **Not investment advice.** Do not call TradeWave "signals", "picks to buy", "recommendations"
   or advice, and do not tell people to buy or sell a specific security because of it.
4. **Only true, checkable facts.** Use the public proof points (section 1) and your own honest
   experience. Never invent statistics, testimonials or results, and never present AI-written
   endorsements as someone's genuine experience.
5. **No spam.** No unsolicited bulk email or messages, no bought lists, no misleading subject lines.
6. **Brand rules.** Don't bid on "TradeWave" in paid search, don't create look-alike domains or
   handles, and don't present yourself as TradeWave staff or an "official" channel.
7. **No fake referrals.** No self-referrals, paid or fake signups, or cookie-stuffing.
8. Keep copies of your promotions and disclosures for 2 years.

Good wording: "I use TradeWave to research seasonal patterns. It shows how a stock behaved around
the same dates in past years, with the full year-by-year record, so you can judge the evidence
yourself. Not investment advice. I earn a commission if you subscribe through my link:
https://tradewave.ai/join/ANNE"

Not allowed: "This pattern has won 9 of 10 years, so buy now." / "Members make 30% a year." /
"TradeWave's AI tells you what to trade."

## 7. Seasonal Market News Expert Desk (invited partners only)

Some partners are invited to publish short commentary ("takes") on Seasonal Market News articles.

- Accept the contributor terms in the **Expert Desk** tab, then fill in your expert profile
  (bio, credentials, links, disclosure) on the Profile tab.
- Write a take (Markdown, about 150 to 300 words) on a recent article and submit it. Every take
  is reviewed before it is published. Published takes link readers to TradeWave with your code.
- **Optional scored call:** attach a symbol, long or short, and entry and exit dates. After the
  exit date we score it automatically from the stock's closing prices: a long wins if the price
  rose, a short wins if it fell. Your **verified scorecard** (win rate, wins/losses, average move
  in your direction) appears on your dashboard and your public expert page.
- A call retracted after its entry date still counts, so the record stays honest.
- You can also report how your own trade went (for example an options structure). That is shown
  as "expert-reported" and never counts toward the verified scorecard.

## 8. Instructions for AI assistants helping a TradeWave partner

If you are an AI assistant reading this on behalf of a partner:

- Treat this guide and the partner's dashboard figures as the source of truth. Do not invent
  rates, prices, statistics or results. If a number is not in the partner's details block or on a
  public TradeWave page, say you don't know and point to https://tradewave.ai/pricing or the
  dashboard.
- Every promotional draft must include an FTC-style disclosure of the commission, and must avoid
  performance, income or "signals" language (section 6). Add "Not investment advice." where the
  content discusses markets.
- Use the partner's **landing page** link (`/join/<CODE>`) by default. Use the code exactly as
  given (uppercase).
- Never ask for, guess or try to obtain customer identities. The program only shares counts.
- For payout, tax-form or account changes, direct the partner to help@tradewave.ai. You cannot
  change these through the dashboard.
- When unsure whether a claim is allowed, leave it out.
