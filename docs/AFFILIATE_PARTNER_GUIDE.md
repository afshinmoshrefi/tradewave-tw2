# TradeWave Partner Guide

This guide is for TradeWave affiliate partners and for any AI assistant a partner uses to plan,
write or check their promotion. It explains how the program works, what the partner dashboard
shows, how you get paid, and the rules every promotion must follow.

Your personal details (code, links, customer discount, renewal term, payout method) are on your
dashboard at https://tradewave.ai/account/affiliate. The **Promote** page there has a "Copy for my AI"
button that gives you this guide with your own details filled in at the top.

This guide describes **program version 2** (agreement version 2026-11-24). The dollar examples use
round prices to keep the arithmetic easy; they are not TradeWave's actual prices.

If this guide and your signed Affiliate Agreement ever disagree, **the Agreement wins**.

---

## 1. What TradeWave is (so you can describe it accurately)

- TradeWave is a **research tool** that finds seasonal patterns: price moves that have repeated
  around the same dates in past years, across 15 markets (US, Europe, Asia, futures, FX, crypto).
- Every pattern shows its evidence: year-by-year results, win rate, risk statistics and an
  ML win-probability score, so the reader can judge it for themselves.
- The daily AI pick has a public, forward-tested track record at https://tradewave.ai/scorecard.
- New accounts get a free 7-day trial of the full product. Current plans and prices are at
  https://tradewave.ai/pricing (prices change; always link there instead of quoting numbers).
- TradeWave is **educational and analytical information only**. It is not investment advice,
  not "signals", not recommendations, and it does not predict the market.

## 2. Your code, links and the customer discount

You have **one referral code** (for example `ANNE`). Your audience gets your discount and you get
credit when they use any of these:

| What you share | Example | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Your landing page | `https://tradewave.ai/join/ANNE` | A ready-made page with your name, photo and note, your discount, and a subscribe button. Best for most posts. |
| Your referral link | `https://tradewave.ai/?code=ANNE` | The TradeWave home page with your discount applied at checkout. |
| Any TradeWave page | add `?code=ANNE` to the URL | Works the same way. |
| Your code itself | `ANNE` typed at checkout | Gives the discount and credits you. |

- **60-day window, first click wins.** The first time someone opens your link we store a cookie
  for 60 days. If they subscribe within 60 days, the sale is credited to you, even if they come
  back later through a plain link. If they had already clicked another partner's link first,
  that partner keeps the credit unless your code is used at checkout.
- **You choose the discount**, from 0% to 25% (section 4). It applies to the customer's first year
  (one annual payment, or the first 12 monthly payments). After that they pay the regular price.
- **No self-referrals.** Subscriptions bought with your own email or account never earn
  commission.

## 3. How you earn

There are three kinds of commission. All of them are calculated **before tax**; sales tax and VAT
are never part of the numbers.

### 3.1 First year: the 50% pool

For every customer you refer, **50% of the regular list price is yours to split** between a
discount for the customer and commission for you. Whatever you give the customer comes out of
your half. TradeWave always keeps the other 50% of the list price.

**You earn (50% minus your discount) of the list price.** On a $100 plan:

| Your discount | Customer pays | You earn | TradeWave keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | $100 | $50 | $50 |
| 5% | $95 | $45 | $50 |
| 20% | $80 | $30 | $50 |
| 25% (the maximum) | $75 | $25 | $50 |

- On an **annual** plan this is the one payment for the first year.
- On a **monthly** plan it applies to each of the first 12 monthly payments.
- Tax does not change it: if $8 of tax is added to a $100 sale at 0% off, the customer pays $108
  and you still earn $50.
- A sale with no partner attached earns no commission for anyone.

### 3.2 Renewals: 25% of what the customer pays

From the customer's second year you earn the **renewal rate, 25% of what the customer actually
pays** at each renewal, for the renewal term in your agreement, as long as you meet the activity
rule (section 6).

| Renewal | Customer pays | You earn |
|---|---|---|
| $399 annual renewal, years 2 to 5 | $399 | $99.75 |
| $399 annual renewal, year 6 or later, on a 5-year term | $399 | $0 |
| $399 annual renewal, any later year, on a lifetime term | $399 | $99.75 |
| Any renewal while you are not active (section 6) | $399 | $0 (Lapsed) |

- **Your renewal term** is in Exhibit A of your agreement and on your dashboard. It is one of:
  **years 2 to 5** (the standard term), **years 2 to 10**, or **lifetime**.
- On a 5-year term an annual customer has four renewals that can pay you (years 2, 3, 4 and 5).
  A monthly customer pays you the first-year rate in months 1 to 12, the renewal rate in months
  13 to 60, and nothing from month 61.
- **Year 1 starts at the customer's first real payment.** A free trial or a $0 invoice does not
  start the clock.
- Renewal commission is 25% of what the customer **actually pays**, so a customer who renews at a
  lower price earns you 25% of that lower price.

### 3.3 Company-wide promotions

Sometimes TradeWave runs a sale for new customers, for example "first year for $99".

- **Your visitor gets the lower price, never both.** If the sale price is lower than the price
  with your discount, they get the sale price. If your discount gives the lower price (or the
  same price), they get your discount. The two are never stacked.
- **You keep the referral credit either way.** The customer is still yours for renewals.
- **If the sale price applies, you earn 50% of what the customer paid** in the first year,
  in place of the pool calculation:

| Sale | Customer pays | You earn | TradeWave keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referred $99 promotion | $99 | $49.50 | $49.50 |
| Referred $50 promotion | $50 | $25 | $25 |
| $99 promotion with no partner | $99 | $0 | $99 |

- If your discount applies, section 3.1 applies as usual.
- After the promotional first year the customer renews at the regular price and section 3.2
  applies.
- Promotions are for new customers only. Customers on older legacy pricing cannot switch to a
  promotion price.
- You do not need to do anything. Checkout compares the two prices automatically, and your
  landing page shows the offer your visitor will get.

### 3.4 What never earns commission

Taxes, free trials, 100%-off periods, refunded or disputed payments, your own purchases, renewals
after your renewal term, and renewals paid while you are not active.

## 4. Changing your customer discount

1. Sign in to your dashboard and open **Settings**.
2. Pick any discount from **0% to 25%**. The page shows what the customer will pay and what you
   will earn before you save. You can set annual and monthly plans separately.
3. Save. The new discount is live for your link, your landing page and your typed code.

What to know:

- **It only affects new checkouts.** Anyone who starts checkout after you save gets the new
  discount. Customers who already subscribed keep the discount they got, and your commission on
  them stays as it was.
- **No re-signing and no pause.** Changing your discount does not change your agreement.
- **One change every 24 hours.** The dashboard tells you when the next change is available.
- **0% is allowed.** Your link and code still credit you; the customer simply pays list price and
  you keep the full 50%.
- Your change history is kept and shown on the dashboard.
- Update any post that quotes your old discount. A promise of a discount that checkout no longer
  gives is a misleading claim (section 9).

## 5. Returning customers

- A customer of yours who cancels and comes back **within 12 months** is still your customer. The
  year count carries on from their original start, so they do not get a second "first year".
- A customer who comes back **after more than 12 months** is a brand-new customer. They belong to
  whichever partner refers them then, or to no one.
- A returning customer does not count as a new paying customer for the activity rule.

## 6. The activity rule (renewals only)

Renewal commission is for partners who are still bringing in customers.

**The rule:** a renewal pays you only if you brought in **at least 1 new paying customer in the
12 months before that renewal payment, plus a 30-day grace period**.

- "New paying customer" means a referred customer's **first successful payment above $0**. Free
  trials do not count until the customer actually pays.
- The rule applies to **renewals only**. First-year commission (sections 3.1 and 3.3) is never
  affected by it.
- A renewal paid while you are not active earns $0 and shows as **Lapsed**. It is **forfeited**:
  it is not paid later, even after you become active again.
- Renewals **start paying again** with the first renewal after your next new paying customer.

**Example timeline** (one annual customer at $399, 0% discount, 5-year term):

| Date | What happens | You earn |
|---|---|---|
| 15 Jan 2027 | Customer A makes their first payment. You are active. | $199.50 (first year) |
| 15 Jan 2028 | A renews (year 2). Your last new paying customer was A, 12 months ago. That is inside 12 months plus the 30-day grace. | $99.75 |
| About 14 Feb 2028 | Grace ends. No new paying customer since January 2027, so you are no longer active. | |
| 15 Jan 2029 | A renews (year 3). You are not active. | $0 (Lapsed, forfeited) |
| 3 Mar 2029 | Customer B makes their first payment. You are active again. | First-year commission on B |
| 15 Jan 2030 | A renews (year 4). B joined 10 months ago. | $99.75 |
| 15 Jan 2031 | A renews (year 5). Paid only if you brought in someone new after about mid-December 2029. | $99.75 or $0 |
| 15 Jan 2032 | A renews (year 6). Past the 5-year term. | $0 |

The simple way to think about it: **bring in at least one new paying customer every year** and
every renewal inside your term pays. Your dashboard shows whether you are active and the date
your active status runs out.

## 7. Statements, refunds and getting paid

### 7.1 Statement states

Every commission line on your Earnings page has a state:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Estimated** | The current month, still open. It refreshes about once an hour and can go down as well as up (refunds, disputes). Not final. |
| **Pending** | The month has closed and the amount is calculated. It is waiting to be paid, for example for the monthly payout run, for your balance to reach $50, or for your tax form. |
| **Paid** | Included in a payout that has been sent. The payout reference is on your Payouts page. |
| **Held** | Earned while your account was paused. It is kept for you and becomes payable when the account is reinstated. Held amounts are lost only if the agreement is terminated for cause. |
| **Lapsed** | A renewal paid while you did not meet the activity rule (section 6). It earned $0 and is not paid later. |

### 7.2 Refunds and disputes

- If a customer is refunded, or a bank dispute takes the money back, the matching commission is
  taken back **at the same rate it was earned**. It does not matter if you changed your discount
  in between.
- Example: an $80 sale (20% off a $100 plan) earned you $30. A full refund takes back $30.
  A $40 partial refund takes back $15.
- The reversal lands in the month the refund happens, even if the original month was already
  paid. If a month ends negative, the difference is taken off your next payout.

### 7.3 Payouts and the $50 minimum

1. Each month closes on the last day. On the **2nd** of the next month we calculate your
   commission and email you a statement (totals and a customer count, never customer identities).
2. You are paid **monthly by PayPal or Wise** to the account on file.
3. **The payout minimum is $50** per currency. A smaller balance is not lost: it is carried
   forward and paid in the first month your balance reaches $50.
4. **A tax form is required before any payout:** IRS Form W-9 (U.S. persons), W-8BEN (non-U.S.
   individuals) or W-8BEN-E (non-U.S. entities). **Do not email your tax form** - it carries
   your tax ID. Reply to your welcome email or contact help@tradewave.ai and we will send you a
   secure upload link. Your dashboard shows the lines as Held ("Held until we have your tax
   form") until we have it.
5. You are paid in the currency your customers paid in. PayPal or Wise may charge their own fees.
6. To change payout details, contact help@tradewave.ai. Do not email bank details or card
   numbers; if we need more than your PayPal or Wise email we send a secure link. Changes are
   verified by hand because they control real money.
   Payouts are sent by the 15th of the month after the statement month.
7. Questions about a statement must reach us within 30 days of it.

### 7.4 If your account is paused

A pause switches your link and code off for new customers. Commission on your existing customers
keeps adding up as **Held** and is paid once the account is reinstated. A new agreement version
also pauses your account until you sign it.

## 9. Promotion rules (summary of Agreement section 7)

Every promotion, including anything an AI writes for you, must follow these:

1. **Disclose the relationship every time**, clearly and near the link: for example
   "I earn a commission if you subscribe through my link" or "#ad / affiliate link".
2. **No performance or income claims.** Never say or imply that subscribers will make money,
   that TradeWave predicts the market, that a pattern "will" repeat, or that it removes risk.
3. **Not investment advice.** Do not call TradeWave "signals", "picks to buy", "recommendations"
   or advice, and do not tell people to buy or sell a specific security because of it.
4. **Only true, checkable facts.** Use the public proof points (section 1) and your own honest
   experience. Quote your discount only as it is today, and do not promise a promotion price
   unless that promotion is live. Never invent statistics, testimonials or results, and never present AI-written
   endorsements as someone's genuine experience.
5. **No spam.** No unsolicited bulk email or messages, no bought lists, no misleading subject lines.
6. **Brand rules.** Don't bid on "TradeWave" in paid search, don't create look-alike domains or
   handles, and don't present yourself as TradeWave staff or an "official" channel.
7. **No fake referrals.** No self-referrals, paid or fake signups, or cookie-stuffing.
8. Keep copies of your promotions and disclosures for 2 years.

Good wording: "I use TradeWave to research seasonal patterns. It shows how a stock behaved around
the same dates in past years, with the full year-by-year record, so you can judge the evidence
yourself. Not investment advice. I earn a commission if you subscribe through my link:
https://tradewave.ai/join/ANNE"

Not allowed: "This pattern has won 9 of 10 years, so buy now." / "Members make 30% a year." /
"TradeWave's AI tells you what to trade."

## 10. Seasonal Market News Expert Desk (invited partners only)

Some partners are invited to publish short commentary ("takes") on Seasonal Market News articles.

- Accept the contributor terms on the **Expert Desk** page, then fill in your expert profile
  (bio, credentials, links, disclosure) in your dashboard profile.
- Write a take (Markdown, about 150 to 300 words) on a recent article and submit it. Every take
  is reviewed before it is published. Published takes link readers to TradeWave with your code.
- **Optional scored call:** attach a symbol, long or short, and entry and exit dates. After the
  exit date we score it automatically from the stock's closing prices: a long wins if the price
  rose, a short wins if it fell. Your **verified scorecard** (win rate, wins/losses, average move
  in your direction) appears on your dashboard and your public expert page.
- A call retracted after its entry date still counts, so the record stays honest.
- You can also report how your own trade went (for example an options structure). That is shown
  as "expert-reported" and never counts toward the verified scorecard.

## 11. Instructions for AI assistants helping a TradeWave partner

If you are an AI assistant reading this on behalf of a partner:

- Treat this guide and the partner's dashboard figures as the source of truth. Do not invent
  rates, prices, statistics or results. The dollar examples in sections 3, 6 and 7 use round
  example prices; never present them as TradeWave's prices.
- When the partner asks what a discount would earn them, use the pool rule: they earn
  (50% minus their discount) of the list price in the first year, then 25% of what the customer
  pays on renewals inside their renewal term while they are active. Use the renewal term from
  their details block; if it is not there, say so and point to the dashboard.
- Only quote the partner's discount as it is right now. It can change, so tell them to check
  the dashboard before publishing a number. If a number is not in the partner's details block or on a
  public TradeWave page, say you don't know and point to https://tradewave.ai/pricing or the
  dashboard.
- Every promotional draft must include an FTC-style disclosure of the commission, and must avoid
  performance, income or "signals" language (section 9). Add "Not investment advice." where the
  content discusses markets.
- Use the partner's **landing page** link (`/join/<CODE>`) by default. Use the code exactly as
  given (uppercase).
- Never ask for, guess or try to obtain customer identities. The program only shares counts.
- For payout, tax-form or account changes, direct the partner to help@tradewave.ai. Never tell
  a partner to email a tax form or bank details: TradeWave sends a secure upload link. Only the
  customer discount and the landing-page profile can be changed in the dashboard.
- When unsure whether a claim is allowed, leave it out.

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**How much do I earn on a new customer?**
(50% minus your discount) of the regular list price for their first year. At 0% off you earn 50%;
at 20% off you earn 30%.

**What discount should I pick?**
Your choice. A bigger discount can convert more of your audience; a smaller one earns you more per
sale. You can change it whenever you like (once per 24 hours) and see the numbers before saving.

**If I change my discount, what happens to customers I already have?**
Nothing. They keep the discount they got, and you keep the commission rate that went with it.

**Can I give more than 25% off?**
No. 25% is the maximum on every plan.

**A TradeWave sale is running. Do I lose the customer?**
No. Your visitor gets whichever price is lower, you keep the referral credit, and if the sale
price applies you earn 50% of what they paid.

**Can a customer combine my discount with a sale?**
No. They get the lower of the two prices, never both.

**How long do I earn on a customer?**
The first year at the pool rate, then 25% of each renewal payment for your renewal term: years
2 to 5, years 2 to 10, or lifetime. Your term is in your agreement and on your dashboard.

**What does "active" mean?**
You brought in at least 1 new paying customer in the last 12 months (plus a 30-day grace period).
Only renewal commission depends on it.

**I missed the activity rule. Do I get those renewals back later?**
No. A renewal paid while you were not active is Lapsed and stays at $0. Renewals after your next
new paying customer pay normally again.

**Do free trials count toward the activity rule?**
Not until the customer makes a real payment.

**A customer cancelled and came back. Do I still get credit?**
Yes if they return within 12 months, and the year count continues. After 12 months they are
treated as a new customer.

**Why is a line marked Held?**
Your account was paused when it was earned. It is paid once the account is reinstated.

**Why have I not been paid yet?**
The usual reasons: your balance is under $50 (it carries forward), we do not have your tax form,
or the month has not closed yet.

**A customer got a refund. What happens to my commission?**
It is taken back at the rate it was earned, in the month of the refund. A partial refund takes
back the matching part.

**Is my commission calculated on the price with tax?**
No. Always before tax.

**Can I see who my customers are?**
No. We share counts and totals only.

**Can the program terms change?**
Yes, but only for customers who check out after the change. A change never reduces commission you
have already earned. Your signed agreement has the details.
