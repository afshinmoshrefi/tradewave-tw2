# TradeWave New-User Onboarding - Validated Design (trial-first, trust-signaling)
_Output of a design + 5-persona adversarial loop, 2026-06-25. The build spec._

## Philosophy

Outcome before instruction, and proof before polish: every trial user lands on one real result on their OWN ticker in the first minute - a verdict in plain words, with the regime edge and an honest caveat in the same breath - then learns by attacking the engine, not admiring it. A same-session fast track teaches and converts inside one visit; the 7-day arc is the fallback for slower users. The close recommends the cheapest tier their logged usage proves, and refuses to assert "measured" over a number it cannot yet produce. The product shows its work, including its losses; so does the onboarding.

## Welcome Moment

One centered dark InfoPopup ('welcome-trial' dialogType), fired once on first post-subscribe load (cookie tw_welcomed). It does NOT lead with a demo. Headline: "You're In - 7 Days of Full Access". Body, under 35 words: "Type any ticker and I'll score it against ~98 years - good window, bad window, or noise. No card. Your real usage decides which plan (if any) you actually need." A single inline symbol input sits in the card; submitting it routes straight to the Opp Table pre-filtered (2-24;twr>1.50) on THAT symbol and fires the same-session fast track - the first action is the user's own ticker, never a canned reel. Primary button "Score a Stock" (focuses the input); secondary "Just browse" sets tw_welcomed and drops them on their Stocks-Scored view. A visible top-right "Skip tips" control sets tw_onboard_dismissed in one click. Suppressed entirely for Tara-entry and report-bridge users. No confetti, no checklist, no persona question here.

## Personalization

No upfront persona question - it is removed from the welcome and never blocks the wow, and a nervous beginner is never asked to self-identify before they know the terms. Persona is INFERRED from behavior, which is more honest than a survey and is what makes the end-of-trial rec believable: opening a 150-340d hold or using Reverse Date Range once flips the arc to LONG automatically; sub-120d setups and Opp-Table filtering flip it SHORT; the genuine default for an unknown user is NEUTRAL (full arc, no short flavor). The only optional ask appears AFTER the first result lands, as one dismissible Tara chip when behavior is still ambiguous: "Mostly holding for months, or trading over weeks? Either's fine - skip if unsure." Three chips: Months / Weeks / Show me both. It re-weights tip emphasis and routes the loaded workflow (LONG leads with the 100-Year Pattern as a 9-month positioning hold + Reverse Date Range; SHORT leads with Opp Table -> Wave Viewer + stop/target discipline). It NEVER gates content. Tara-entry and report-bridge users are seeded heuristically from their question/tickers, so they are never asked.

## Day By Day

TWO tracks off one engine. SAME-SESSION FAST TRACK (primary): the moment a user opens the Opp Table or scores a symbol in their first visit, Tara compresses D1-D3 into inline narration ON THE SETUP THEY ARE LOOKING AT - pattern definition, scan-vs-validate, win-rate-WITH-the-smoothness-score - no waiting for tomorrow, and a persistent 'Why this beats a normal scanner' link opens the PE+2 / 100-Year-Pattern explainer on demand so the differentiator is reachable out of sequence in one session. DAILY ARC (fallback for users who leave and return): one ~15-second Tara card per calendar day via the existing tip scheduler (new localStorage tw_onboard_day seeded at first login, guard tw_onboard_lastfired != today; welcome-close sets tw_tara_tip_index='1' so the 90s tip never double-fires). Every metric term gets a 5-word plain gloss the first time it appears (e.g. "TWR - how smooth the ride was"). D1 leads with WHY, then the verdict, then the schema: "Some calendar windows have paid off almost every year for a century - here's yours," a one-line plain verdict on the chart ("paid off 19 of the last 24 years - rare"), a one-line PE+2 tease in the SAME result ("calibrated to the election cycle - 2026 is PE+2"), and the 5-part pattern definition LAST. D2 the SCAN->VALIDATE->ORGANIZE->ACT loop + the three fixed Wave-Viewer views in order; plant "ranked is not approved". D3 read win-rate WITH the smoothness score + sample honesty, and the DO FORCES the lesson: it opens a deliberately thin sample so the user reads a 100%-on-3-years and gets told why it's noise; the Green/Yellow/Red Confidence Overlay debuts here; for LONG-persona users D3 also foregrounds path-risk (MAE / max-drawdown / "how deep underwater before it worked"). D4 regime/PE+2 - toggle the filter live, watch the same setup flip, AND a paired multiple-comparisons card: "We let you scan thousands of windows - here's how we keep that from manufacturing a 96%" (out-of-sample / hold-out cycles), confronting p-hacking head-on. D5 the 100-Year Pattern, framed by persona: SHORT gets it + stop/target-before-entry discipline; LONG gets it as a 9-month positioning hold with its drawdown profile. D6 the long workflow (Buy & Hold -> Reverse Date Range -> 150-340d hold + PE positioning), expanded to a genuine two-beat for LONG/inferred-long users so the higher-LTV buyer is never the B-track. D7 consolidate -> save one pattern + set the reminder, then the conversion card. Tier-aware: any DO referencing an out-of-reach market swaps to an in-tier ticker; days already covered by the user's own actions skip; example tickers are clamped to a NEUTRAL baseline (not steered toward high-tier futures/crypto) so the close can't be read as a funnel. Miss a day, it waits, never stacks; ignored two days, it auto-softens to every-other-day; two fast dismisses by a self-server, it goes auto-OFF (Tara on demand only).

## Days Remaining Treatment

Two calm, never-blocking surfaces, at most one mention per day, and NEVER shown unless an affirmative future trial-end is present (default-to-silent on ambiguous state, so a payer never sees a clock). (1) A small monochrome capsule on the Tara launcher: "Day 3 of 7" (hover "4 days of full access left"), driven by window.tw2_trial_ends_at for the reverse trial; paid trials require the missing tw2_paid_trial_ends_at field on the token. (2) The same line in the daily card header: "Full Access - 4 Days Left". It counts what they still HAVE, neutral, no red, no ticking clock. From D5 it gains a soft amber tint and pairs ONCE with a loss-plant that is gated on genuine comprehension/usage: it fires the specific version ("the PE+2 window on $NVDA you flagged is Navigator-only after the trial") only if the user demonstrably used PE+2 on that ticker; otherwise it falls back to plain language ("the seasonal scores on the stocks you looked at go away"). For LONG users the plant speaks to positioning, not setups ("the Reverse Date Range hold and 98-year history you used on $SPY drop to Explorer's Dow-30"). Tapping the pill opens Tara's usage summary - the same trust-signaled view used at trial end - so "what happens after" is one tap away, never forced.

## Tara Introduction

Tara is introduced by RESULT, not a "meet your assistant" screen, and never by claiming she did the thinking for you. She is named in the welcome ("Type a ticker and I'll score it") and is LIVE the second the app loads (tip-0 repointed to the day-aware onboarding message; auto-greet opens for trial users rather than waiting 90s). The fast-track DO opens her on the user's OWN scored ticker and she narrates by pointing AT the raw rows, not abstracting them: "Here's every cycle's individual result - read it yourself, I'll just point. This window is PE+2-calibrated; 2026 is PE+2." The "I read 98 years so you don't have to" framing is killed - she foregrounds the per-year table and the reproducible deep-link instead. From D2 on, each daily card hands Tara a fresh pre-filled question (reusing chatbotPrefill -> prefillFiredRef autosend), so she is the single through-line of the curriculum. The Confidence Overlay debuts on D3. Strongest intro of all is the homepage entry, where she is already mid-answer before any click.

## Homepage Tara Entry

A user who typed into "See the Evidence" arrives via /signup?next=/app/?ask=Q; App.js reads ?ask=, opens Tara, and auto-sends their exact question (already built). Their question IS the Day-1 wow, so the welcome modal and the canned example are SUPPRESSED - we never talk over the answer they came for. A one-line in-chat banner above Tara's reply: "You're in - 7 days of full access. Here's the evidence you asked for." Tara answers with real per-year stats, drives the chart to it, names the regime in the same answer ("2026 is PE+2 - that's why this window looks the way it does"), and - on-brand for the honesty - links the one cycle that FAILED rather than only the wins. If the question named a symbol, that becomes their D1 pattern and the arc proceeds from THEIR question; persona is seeded heuristically (when/buy-now -> short; hold/invest/long -> long), so the chips never show. After the answer lands, one inline line converts curiosity into the loop: "That's measurement, not a guess - I never invent the numbers, and I'll show you the one that lost too. Want the same on a stock you own?" The days-remaining pill renders; the fast track is live and the daily arc picks up at D2. Report-bridge users get the parallel treatment: "My Stocks" watchlist active, banner "Your calibrated scores are unlocked," Tara pre-filled to "Which of my stocks has the strongest seasonal window right now - and which doesn't?"

## Paid Tier Variants

Detection is affirmative: window.tw2_user_tier present AND no future trial-end -> paid path; on ambiguous state, default to NO trial pill and NO loss-plant (a payer must never see a "days left" clock or a "you'll lose this" line). A direct payer gets the SAME methodology curriculum (identical at every tier) and the same fast track, with state-correct trimming and NO trial pill, NO loss-plants, NO conversion card. Welcome congratulates the PLAN ("Welcome to Navigator" / "Dow, NASDAQ and S&P 500, AI-scored - score your first stock"). Every example clamps to what the tier can open: Navigator's D4-D6 use its 3 indices + the election-cycle filter and 100-Year Pattern on the S&P 500 it owns; Analyst adds Russell/Wilshire/ETF + deep-history examples (100-pattern framing); Strategist may use a futures/commodity/crypto flagship, highlights AUTO PE-cycle on D4, 500-pattern framing, and runs a thinner arc (they bought the top - teach features, don't sell). No higher tier is ever dangled at a payer. The curriculum runs only for NEW accounts (tw_onboard_day unset); an UPGRADE is not re-onboarded - one "What's New in [Tier]" card naming only the unlocked markets/features. D7 for payers ends on retention only ("You've got the full engine - lock in your first reminder") plus a usage-detected pointer to one or two power features they haven't touched ("you're paying for this - use it"). If a payer's logged usage genuinely implies a tier ABOVE what they bought, Tara notes it ONCE, gently - same honest logic, never a campaign.

## End Of Trial Conversion

FIRES on D7 OR on exit-intent OR after N real validated scans in a heavy single session - so the trust-signal is reachable in the first visit, not hidden behind a week the impatient/on-the-fence user won't spend. One Tara message + a single "Your 7 Days, Measured" card. HONESTY GATE on the word "measured": if the usage event sink is logging, the card asserts measured usage; until it is, the copy hedges truthfully and never claims a number it can't produce - "You spent most of your week on S&P 500 and NASDAQ short-term setups, so Navigator likely covers you" instead of a false "measured." It LEADS with the down-rec - the minimum tier that covers real usage - and names what NOT to buy: "Navigator ($19/mo) covers all of it - you don't need Analyst or Strategist, and we'd rather tell you that than sell you more. I'd skip them." Dow-30-only users: "Honestly, free Explorer is enough for you - keep it, no card." It recommends UP only on evidence the user SOUGHT OUT unprompted (not markets the cards handed them, since example tickers are clamped neutral), and weights HISTORY DEPTH / instrument breadth so a low-frequency deep-history user is routed to Analyst with a concrete reason ("you pulled 90+ year context on 3 instruments - Navigator caps the history you used"), not under-sold to Navigator. The recommended tier is the ONE highlighted button; others are present but secondary, each with a plain "why you don't need this yet." Monthly is the visible default; annual appears ONLY as a quiet "save with yearly" badge - never annual-first, and NO 72h urgency coupon at a first-time skeptic. For the NON-converter, at expiry the tier silently drops to Explorer with one gracious line: "Your full access wrapped up - you're on Explorer now (Dow-30, free, forever). Everything you saved is still here. Upgrade anytime, one click." No countdown guilt, no shaming. The specific loss is named once as a fact, not a threat, and a persistent unobtrusive "Reopen full access" link lives in the header thereafter, restoring the exact tier Tara recommended.

## Dismiss Model

Three gears, respectful. SLOW-DOWN (the beginner gear, new): a one-tap "New to this? Slow it down" on the welcome and any daily card switches to a simpler track - more analogy, fewer acronyms, every-other-day pacing, no persona chip. PER-DAY: every card has an X and "Got it" - closing sets tw_onboard_lastfired=today (it never reappears that day; tomorrow's still comes; the DO is optional and dismissing still counts the day as taught). Opening Tara yourself before a card fires cancels that day's card. PERMANENT: a visible top-right "Skip tips" on the welcome (the same control as "Just browse" can set it) AND a one-time checkbox on any daily card -> cookie tw_onboard_dismissed, the whole arc stops instantly, no confirm dialog. AUTO-OFF: a confirmed self-server (opened Tara unprompted, then two fast dismisses) is switched fully silent automatically - launcher pulse killed, Tara on demand only - rather than only softened. Mute leaves Tara fully usable and does NOT suppress the days-remaining pill or the end-of-trial card (those are state facts, not tips). Re-enable via "Restart the tour" in Tara's help. Welcome and conversion cards are cookie-gated one-time and never re-fire.

## Look And Feel

Premium dark, native via themeColors(UITheme): bg rgb(15,10,21), panels rgb(30,27,42), light text. The welcome and end-of-trial moments reuse the centered InfoPopup chrome (dark, rounded, generous padding, one accent-gradient primary button, hairline dividers) - no illustrations, no confetti (measurement, not hype). Daily teaching lives in a compact ~320px glass card anchored near Tara's launcher with a thin left accent border so it reads as "from Tara," a 7-segment progress rail across the top, one AP Title Case headline, max two short body lines, one primary button, one quiet dismiss, and a visible top-right "Skip tips". Real chart thumbnails preview each DO so it looks like the product. The days-remaining pill is a tiny monochrome capsule, amber only at D5-7. The Green/Yellow/Red Confidence Overlay is the ONLY saturated color in the whole experience, so it carries meaning. Motion is a single soft fade+rise; the quiet launcher pulse fires ONLY for users not yet flagged self-servers - once someone self-serves it goes fully silent (no pulse = no soft red badge). AP Title Case headlines, no terminal period, no em-dashes (" - " separators). It should feel like a quant terminal that respects your time.

## Why It Wins

It now answers each persona's biggest objection directly. IMPATIENT DAY-TRADER ("I'll never come back for a 7-day course; show me MY ticker, give me the close in this session"): the welcome is a symbol input that drops them on the Opp Table on their own ticker; a same-session fast track compresses D1-D3 inline on the setup they're staring at; the trust-signaled tier rec fires on exit-intent / after N scans, not only D7; "Skip tips" is top-right one-click; a confirmed self-server goes auto-OFF with no launcher pulse; and "Why this beats a normal scanner" surfaces PE+2 out of sequence. LONG-TERM INVESTER ("I'm routed to the B-track, my wow arrives D6, the flagship is mis-shelved"): persona is inferred from behavior and flips LONG automatically; the 100-Year Pattern is re-shelved as a 9-month positioning HOLD for long users; D3 foregrounds MAE/drawdown/path-pain; D6 expands to two beats; the close weights history-depth so Analyst's value is visible, not under-sold; and "position/hold" replaces "setup" in long copy. SKEPTIC ("a pre-loaded winner is a cherry; you never address multiple-comparisons or n=24; the TWR is a black box"): D1 links the cycle that LOST, the engine returns ugly numbers for junk inputs on demand, D4 is an explicit multiple-comparisons/hold-out card, the flagship stat always ships with its caveat and small-n warning, the per-year raw rows are foregrounded over Tara's summary, a data-provenance card states what the series is, and example tickers are clamped neutral so the up-rec can't be an engineered funnel. BEGINNER ("the jargon wall makes me feel dumb; the wow is a number I can't grade; don't make me pick a persona"): every term gets a 5-word plain gloss, the wow leads with a plain verdict before the chart, the persona question is removed/deferred and optional, D1 leads with WHY before the schema, a "Slow it down" third gear exists, and the D5 loss-plant falls back to plain language if comprehension wasn't shown. TOUGH BUYER ("the 'measured' close rides a usage log that doesn't exist; over-serving then down-recommending is anchoring; annual-first re-triggers my alarms"): the close hedges truthfully until the event sink logs and never asserts "measured" over an estimate, the flagship always carries uncertainty framing (practicing its own D3 lesson), annual-first and the 72h coupon are killed (monthly default, quiet yearly badge), state surfaces default-to-silent on ambiguity so a payer never sees a trial clock, and the launch is gated on the scorecard showing real losses as losses - with D1 deliberately linking a loss. Across the rubric it still maxes time-to-wow (own ticker, plain verdict, seconds), comprehension-under-test (DOs force the lesson, including a deliberate thin-sample and a hold-out), unique value short AND long (both first-class), right-tier zero-oversell (cheapest fit, honest hedge, no annual-first), brevity (one card/two lines), look (dark, color reserved), respect (three gears, auto-off, no red badge), state-correctness (affirmative detection, default-silent), and grace (Explorer keeps saved work, names the loss once, one-click reopen) - and it stays buildable on the named integration reality, with the tw_onboard_day counter, the usage event sink, and tw2_paid_trial_ends_at as the only genuinely new pieces, the first of which the copy explicitly refuses to over-claim until it exists.
