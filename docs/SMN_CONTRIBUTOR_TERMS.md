# SMN Contributor Terms

Version: 2026-07-07

These terms govern participation in the Seasonal Market News (SMN) Expert Desk,
the optional program that lets invited TradeWave partners publish commentary on
SMN articles. They are in addition to your TradeWave Affiliate Agreement, which
remains unchanged. Accepting these terms is required only if you choose to
participate in the Expert Desk.

## 1. What You Publish

You may submit short written commentary ("takes") attached to SMN articles:
your interpretation of the data and, if you choose, how you would approach the
trade. Every take is reviewed by TradeWave before publication. TradeWave may
decline or remove any take at its discretion.

## 2. Impersonal Commentary Only

Your takes are general, impersonal market commentary addressed to no specific
person. You will not provide individualized investment advice, address any
reader's personal situation, or solicit or manage funds through the Expert
Desk. Takes must not state or imply that TradeWave endorses your trades.

## 3. Disclosures

- If you hold or intend to open a position in an instrument you write about,
  you must say so in the take or in your standing disclosure line.
- Your relationship with TradeWave is disclosed automatically: every take
  carries a notice that you earn a commission on referred subscriptions.
- You will not accept compensation from any issuer, promoter, or third party
  in exchange for coverage of a security, and you will not write about
  securities with a market capitalization under 300 million USD.

## 4. The Scorecard

If you declare a scored call (symbol, direction, entry and exit dates),
TradeWave records it and scores it at the close of the window from underlying
prices, using the same rule the TradeWave engine applies to pattern win rates.
Scored calls and their outcomes are public and permanent. Trade structure
details and outcomes you report yourself are displayed as your own reporting,
clearly separated from TradeWave's measured record.

## 5. License and Attribution

You grant TradeWave a non-exclusive, worldwide license to publish, format,
excerpt, and archive your takes and profile on SMN and TradeWave properties,
with attribution to you. You may republish your own takes elsewhere after they
appear on SMN. You may retract a published take at any time from your
dashboard; retraction removes it from articles but scored-call records remain.

## 6. Standards

Takes must be your own work, factually careful, and free of profanity,
personal attacks, and undisclosed promotion. TradeWave may edit only for
formatting; wording changes are sent back to you instead.

## 7. Ending Participation

You may pause or stop participating at any time; TradeWave may do the same.
Ending participation does not affect your Affiliate Agreement, published
scored-call history, or commissions already earned.

## 8. No Advice, No Guarantee

SMN content and your takes are for information and education. Nothing in the
Expert Desk is investment, legal, or tax advice. Trading involves risk of
loss. Past performance, measured or reported, does not guarantee future
results.
