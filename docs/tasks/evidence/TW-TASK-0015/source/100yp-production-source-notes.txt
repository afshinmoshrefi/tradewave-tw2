PRODUCTION / ACCURACY NOTES

- Core positioning: TradeWave is a historical analytics machine, not a prediction machine.
- TradeWave reports how comparable historical windows behaved. The user must interpret those results alongside current trend, risk, regime, and outside evidence.
- Do not call 96% the probability that the 2026 cycle will win. It is the historical share of positive completed cycles.
- Do not describe the result as independently validated. The historical cycles are in-sample.
- TradeWave searches many possible calendar windows. This creates a multiple-testing or "look-elsewhere" limitation: unusually strong historical results can emerge partly because a very large search was performed.
- Observation 25 begins to add prospective evidence under a locked rule. One new observation cannot eliminate the look-elsewhere problem or independently validate the historical result.
- A positive 2026-2027 result would be consistent with the historical baseline; it would not prove that TradeWave predicted the result.
- A small negative finish would be an ordinary statistical failure and would not, by itself, prove structural change.
- Do not use the book or TradeWave buy-and-hold comparison in narration. The public methodology explains that the annual-period buy-and-hold figure is not a like-for-like September-to-July benchmark.
- A drawdown beyond approximately 20% would be outside every prior winning cycle. If the decline were deep and persistent, it could be evidence of a structural regime change; it would not prove another Great Depression.
- The exact reported worst MAE among the 23 winning cycles is 2018 at -19.47%. The threshold is therefore stated as "roughly minus 20 percent" and explicitly tied to TradeWave's reported record.
- The fixed exit close determines the official positive or negative result, while MFE and MAE remain essential for interpreting the path.
- Do not invent personal-story details for drama. The book says the chart was saved, reopened about an hour later, reopened again, and repeatedly checked; it does not say there was a week-long bug hunt.
- Do not imply that the measured windows themselves included Black Monday, the 2008 crash, or the pandemic crash. Those events occurred during the broader historical span but outside the relevant fixed windows.
- This content is educational and historical, not investment advice.
