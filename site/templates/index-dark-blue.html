Machine Learning With One Honest Job: Ranking

One scan sweeps all 15 markets and surfaces the setups entering their seasonal window now. A separate gradient-boosted model reads 62 features of every qualifying setup and produces a ranking used for exactly one thing: ordering the list.

It does not promise outcomes. The statistics stay deterministic - the model only decides what you look at first. A no-pattern result is a real finding, not weak support. Once a day, that ranking plus rules selects a single pick, and it is logged in public before the outcome is known, losses included.

/methodology - what we compute, how, and its limits

Deterministic seasonal statistics from end-of-day data across 15 markets, over any 1 to 99 year lookback, calendar or election cycle. Two separate published metrics, never blended: held-to-close win rate and target-hit rate, both over the same set of resolved picks. The model only ranks; it predicts nothing. Every figure traces to a per-year table you can open, and anyone, including an AI agent, can audit the live record.

Not investment advice, and past results never guarantee future ones. We don't ask you to take the claim on faith - the evidence and the record are public, so you can check every number yourself.
