The AI publishes a predicted gain for every pick before the window opens. A pick wins when the move reaches that predicted gain inside the window - the prediction did what it said - or when it closes profitable. Held to close is shown beside it as the stricter read: the share still in profit at the window close. Every row keeps its realized close return visible, so a pick that hit its target and then faded shows both facts.

Every pick was published on X (@TradeWave) before the market opened, timestamp and all - check any call against the tape. Educational research, not investment advice. How these numbers are computed: methodology.
