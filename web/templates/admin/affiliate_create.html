What this discount means in dollars

Each partner has a pool of the list price (50% on the standard terms). The customer discount comes out of the pool and the partner earns the rest: for a $100 plan at 20% off the customer pays $80, the partner earns $30 and TradeWave retains $50. The discount can be 0% up to the maximum of the terms version (25% on the standard terms).
